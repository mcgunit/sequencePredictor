## Game structure

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Transnational draw lottery (organised via SLE), common prize pool |
| Matrix | 6 of 40 + 1 of 5 ("Dream" number) → 19,191,900 combinations |
| Price | €2.50 per combination; €1.30 (52%) goes to the common prize money |
| Draws | Monday and Thursday evening; electronic or physical draw |
| Tiers | 6 (tiers 1, 2 and 6 fixed; tiers 3–5 pari-mutuel) |

## Prize tiers

| Tier | Match | Odds 1 in | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 + Dream | 19,191,900 | €7,200,000 paid as €20,000/month for 30 years |
| 2 | 6 | 4,797,975 | €120,000 paid as €2,000/month for 5 years |
| 3 | 5 | 18,815.59 | Pari-mutuel: 10.32% of remaining prize money |
| 4 | 4 | 456.14 | Pari-mutuel: 28.93% of remaining prize money |
| 5 | 3 | 32.07 | Pari-mutuel: 60.75% of remaining prize money |
| 6 | 2 | 5.52 | Fixed €2.50 (= stake back) |

- Overall odds of any prize: 1 in 4.66.
- The Dream number only matters for tiers 1 and 2.

## Money flow

- 52% of stakes (€1.30 per combination) = prize money.
- **45.21% of the prize money** goes to the common **Reserve Fund**, which pays tiers 1 and 2. Unused money stays in the fund (for Event draws and promotions).
- From what remains, the fixed €2.50 of tier 6 is paid first; the rest is split over tiers 3–5 (10.32 / 28.93 / 60.75%).

## Caps and special cases

- **More than 3 tier 1 winners:** they share a lump sum capped at €21,600,000 (= 3 × €7.2M), paid at once.
- **More than 12 tier 2 winners:** they share a lump sum capped at €1,440,000 (= 12 × €120,000), paid at once.
- If a lower tier would pay more than a higher one, the tiers are pooled (tier 5/6, 4/5, 3/4). Tier 6 can therefore fall below €2.50.
- **No winner in tier 3 or 4:** amount moves to the next lower tier of the same draw. **No winner in tier 5:** amount goes to the Reserve Fund.
- **Event draw / "Boosted annuity" draw:** lotteries can boost the tier 1 and/or tier 2 annuity. A boosted tier 1 can be €10,800,000 (€30,000/month for 30 years; lump-sum cap €32,400,000 above 3 winners). The boosted amount stays until won.

## Computed RTP (our calculation)

- 52% nominal.
- Tier 6 alone: €2.50 / 5.52 ≈ €0.45 per €2.50 → ≈ 18.1%.
- The annuities are nominal amounts paid over 5 or 30 years, so their present value is substantially lower than the headline figure.

## EV research notes

- **Structurally the least promising game for positive EV:** the top prizes are fixed (no growing jackpot), so EV cannot spike with rollovers. The only exceptions are Boosted annuity / Event draws.
- Prize sharing only matters for tiers 3–5 (pari-mutuel) and when tier 1/2 winner counts exceed the caps.
- For the EV calculator, tier 1 and 2 values should be converted to **present value** using a discount rate; this is a design decision to document.
