## Game structure

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | National draw lottery |
| Matrix | 6 of 45 + 1 bonus number (7 balls drawn, the 7th is the bonus) |
| Price | €1.50 per combination (includes the code draw, if organised) |
| Draws | Wednesday and Saturday; extra "special draws" possible |
| Tiers | 9 (tiers 1–6 pari-mutuel, tiers 7–9 fixed) |

## Prize tiers

| Tier | Match | Odds 1 in | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (Jackpot) | 6 | 8,145,060 | 17% of stakes into the "Guarantee Fund for tier 1"; minimum guaranteed jackpot €1,000,000 |
| 2 | 5 + bonus | 1,357,510 | 2.46% |
| 3 | 5 | 35,723.95 | 2.33% |
| 4 | 4 + bonus | 14,289.58 | 1.17% |
| 5 | 4 | 772.41 | 2.16% |
| 6 | 3 + bonus | 579.31 | 1.35% |
| 7 | 3 | 48.28 | Fixed €7.50 |
| 8 | 2 + bonus | 64.37 | Fixed €4.50 |
| 9 | 1 + bonus | 18.39 | Fixed €1.50 (= stake back) |
| "Jackpot fund" (Fonds de cagnotte) | – | – | 3.18% |

- Overall odds of any prize: 1 in 10.67.
- Only the highest tier counts per combination.
- If a pari-mutuel prize in tiers 2–6 is higher than a prize in a higher tier, the tiers are pooled and split equally.
- If a prize in tiers 1–6 would be below €7.50, it is raised to €7.50 from the jackpot fund.
- Rounding: tier 1 up to the euro; tiers 2–6 down to €0.10.

## Rollover and roll-down rules

- **No tier 1 winner:** the tier 1 amount goes to tier 1 of the next draw, **plus €500,000** from the tier 1 Guarantee Fund.
- **No winner in tiers 2–6:** that amount is **kept by the Nationale Loterij** (it does *not* roll down or over).
- **Roll Down (discretionary):** the Nationale Loterij *may* apply a roll-down to any draw once the jackpot has reached an amount of its choosing. If tier 1 is not won, the tier 1 amount goes to the highest lower tier with at least one winner, but not lower than tier 6. If tier 6 has no winner either, it goes back to the Guarantee Fund. The roll-down is announced before the draw.
- The Nationale Loterij may move money between the jackpot fund and the Guarantee Fund and inject any amount it deems necessary.

## Promotions and special draws

- Promotions can increase prizes, financed from the jackpot fund, up to €25M per draw (up to €30M for special draws).
- Promotions can also raise the tier 1 amount, financed from the Guarantee Fund (max €25M per draw), partly to avoid accumulating balances in that fund.
- **Special draws** (extra draws with a commercial name): if tier 1 is not won, the amount either goes to the Guarantee Fund or rolls down (not below tier 6), at the Nationale Loterij's choice.
- **Code draw:** each combination gets a code (4 letters starting with "L", or "E" for special draws, + 5 digits). Prizes guaranteed to be won, each ≥ €1, total ≥ €20,000 per code draw; financed by a variable share of stakes and/or the jackpot fund.

## Computed RTP (our calculation)

- Pari-mutuel allocations: 17 + 2.46 + 2.33 + 1.17 + 2.16 + 1.35 + 3.18 = **29.65%** of stakes.
- Fixed tiers per €1.50: €7.50/48.28 + €4.50/64.37 + €1.50/18.39 ≈ €0.307 → **≈ 20.45%**.
- Total ≈ **50.1%**, plus the code draw (variable).

## EV research notes

- **Most relevant rule:** the discretionary **Roll Down**. When announced, the jackpot flows to tiers 2–6 if not won, a direct analogue of Cash WinFall. Track announcements and check historical roll-down draws in Step 6.
- The +€500,000 per unwon draw and the €1M minimum jackpot mean early-cycle jackpots are partly funded by the Guarantee Fund.
- Unwon tier 2–6 money is retained by the operator, so there is no lower-tier accumulation to exploit.
- Belgium-only game: sales and winner counts are national, which makes the sales and popularity models (Steps 4–5) simpler than for EuroMillions.
