## Game structure

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | National draw lottery, standalone or added to another draw game |
| Combination | 6-digit number (000000–999999) + zodiac sign (12 signs) → 12,000,000 combinations |
| Number choice | **The 6-digit number is always generated by the Nationale Loterij's system (algorithm); the player can only choose the zodiac sign.** Numbers on one ticket are all different. |
| Price | €1.50 per combination |
| Limits | Max 24 combinations per draw and 35 draws per form |
| Draws | Daily; one Joker+ draw per day even if linked to several games |
| Draw method | 7 drums (6 × digits 0–9, 1 × zodiac), or 2 drums with replacement, or electronic |

## Prizes (all fixed)

| Prize | Condition |
|---|---|
| €200,000 | Full match: 6 digits + zodiac sign |
| €20,000 | 6 digits ("winning group" of six) |
| €2,000 | First 5 digits **or** last 5 digits |
| €200 | First 4 **or** last 4 digits |
| €20 | First 3 **or** last 3 digits |
| €5 | First 2 **or** last 2 digits |
| €2 | First digit **or** last digit |
| €1.50 | Zodiac sign (except the full-match combination) |

- Prizes **can be cumulated** across winning groups (e.g. a left group and a right group), but within one group only the highest prize counts.
- **Cap:** total for full-match combinations is limited to €1,000,000 per draw; with more than five full-match winners, they share €1M equally.

## Promotional amount (21st of each month)

- The draw on the **21st of each month** carries a promotional amount between €2,500 and €1,000,000 (set by the Nationale Loterij), added to the full-match prize.
- If not won, it carries over to the **next Joker+ draw with at least one full-match winner**.
- If unwon for several months, the monthly promotional amounts **accumulate**, up to a cap set by the Nationale Loterij of at most **€3,000,000**. Once the cap is reached, new monthly amounts are suspended until it is won.
- Financed by 1%–10% of Joker+ stakes; surplus goes to the "Joker+ pot fund", shortfalls are taken from it.
- **Other promotions:** the Nationale Loterij can raise the fixed prizes by **up to 300%**, funded by the Joker+ pot fund.
- Rounding when sharing: up to the next multiple of €100.

## Computed RTP (our calculation)

- Base game: **≈ €0.718 per €1.50 ≈ 47.9%**.
  - Assumption: left and right groups cumulate; a 6-digit match is one group worth €20,000 (not cumulated with partial groups). Verify against the operator's published odds/prize table.
- Effect of the promotional amount per ticket (odds 1 in 12,000,000): €1M adds ≈ €0.083; the €3M cap adds ≈ €0.25.

## EV research notes

- **Promotional jackpot alone is not enough:** even at the €3M cap, EV ≈ €0.718 + €0.25 ≈ €0.97 < €1.50 (before considering the €1M sharing rule for multiple full-match winners).
- **The "+300% prizes" promotion is the interesting rule:** if all fixed prizes were raised by the maximum, the base EV would exceed the €1.50 stake (≈ €2.15 if it means ×3, ≈ €2.87 if it means +300% = ×4). The exact meaning of "verhogen met maximum 300%" must be checked, and such promotions need to be tracked historically.
- Because the player cannot choose the digits, covering strategies (buying many specific combinations) are impossible; you can only buy many random combinations.
- Fixed prizes mean no prize sharing below the jackpot, so player popularity models are irrelevant here.
